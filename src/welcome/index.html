Welcome to PhishLocal

PhishLocal checks your emails for phishing with the AI built into Chrome and explains every red flag. Your emails never leave your browser.

How to start

  1. 1

    Pin PhishLocal
    Click the puzzle piece in Chrome's toolbar, then the pin next to PhishLocal. Its icon opens the panel.

  2. 2

    Open an email in Gmail
    While the panel is open, PhishLocal checks every email you open, on this device.

  3. 3

    Read the result
    A verdict, every red flag in plain words, and what to do next.

How your privacy is kept

  • Checked on this device
    Basic checks plus Gemini Nano, the AI built into Chrome. Works offline.

  • No email is saved
    Close the panel and all reading stops.

  • Cloud only if you ask
    Personal data is removed first, and you see what is sent.
